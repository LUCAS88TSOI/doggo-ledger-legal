狗狗記賬 (Doggo Ledger) — Privacy Policy / 私隱政策 / 隐私政策

Last updated / 最後更新 / 最后更新: 16 October 2026

English

Doggo Ledger ("the App") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, how we use and protect it, where it is processed, and your rights. It applies to users in every country or region where the App is offered, including Hong Kong, Macau, Taiwan, Malaysia and Singapore.

1. Who we are

  • The App is operated by its independent developer ("we", "us"), who decides how your personal data is used. Contact: aspirineqq8@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Account: the email address you provide if you choose to sign in; otherwise an anonymous account identifier is created automatically.
  • Ledger data: the expense / income amounts, currencies, categories, titles, notes, dates and savings goals you enter.
  • Receipt images: photos you capture or select to upload.
  • Voice clips: audio you record to log expenses.
  • AI text inputs: transaction descriptions you import, bank-message and payment-app notification text you optionally forward for parsing, and aggregate spending figures used to generate short in-app summaries.
  • Preferences: e.g. your bookkeeping currency, daily reminders, haptics.

3. How we use it

  • To provide the core bookkeeping, spending-statistics and pet-raising features.
  • To recognise receipt images and voice via Google Gemini, and to parse or classify optional text inputs via OpenRouter and the downstream AI provider selected by our OpenRouter preset.
  • To sync your data across devices once you sign in.
  • We only use your data for these purposes. If you do not provide certain data (for example, you never upload a receipt), the related feature simply will not be available; the rest of the App still works.

4. Sharing

  • Cloud storage & processing: the App uses Supabase for backend storage. Receipt images and voice clips are sent to Google Gemini. Text needed for SMS and payment-notification parsing, description classification and short spending summaries is sent to OpenRouter, which routes it to a downstream AI model provider selected by our preset. Each provider processes and may retain data according to its own privacy and retention policies.
  • We do not sell your personal data and do not use it for cross-app advertising tracking.
  • We do not disclose your data to any other third party except the service providers above, or where required by law.

5. Where your data is processed (cross-border transfer)

  • Your data is stored on Supabase servers in Singapore. AI recognition requests are processed by Google and by OpenRouter and its downstream providers, which may operate servers in the United States or other countries.
  • This means your data may be transferred outside the country or region where you live. We only transfer the data needed for the feature you use, over encrypted connections, to providers bound by their own data-protection terms.

6. Storage & security

  • Your data is protected by Row Level Security — each user can access only their own data.
  • All network traffic uses HTTPS / TLS encryption.
  • Analysis results of automatic logging (e.g. amount, merchant, last four card digits, category — not the message text itself) are stored with your account so they can be retrieved again after a network interruption, and are deleted when you delete your account.

7. Retention & deletion

  • We keep your data until you delete it or delete your account.
  • You can permanently delete your account and all related data (ledger, receipts, voice, etc.) at any time via Settings → Delete Account. This is irreversible.
  • You can also delete individual records.

8. Children

  • The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

9. Your rights

  • You may request to access, obtain a copy of, correct, supplement or delete your personal data, and to withdraw your consent or ask us to stop using it. Email us at the address below; we will reply within the period required by applicable law (normally within 30 days). Withdrawing consent may mean some features can no longer be provided.
  • Depending on where you live, these rights are also given to you by local law, including:
    • Hong Kong: the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486).
    • Taiwan: the Personal Data Protection Act (個人資料保護法).
    • Malaysia: the Personal Data Protection Act 2010.
    • Singapore: the Personal Data Protection Act 2012.
  • You may also lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

10. Updates

  • We may update this policy from time to time; material changes will be posted in the App or on this page.

11. Contact

  • Privacy questions and requests: aspirineqq8@gmail.com

  • 雲端儲存與處理： 本應用程式使用 Supabase 作為後端儲存。單據圖片與語音會傳送至 Google Gemini；簡訊與付款通知解析、描述分類及簡短支出摘要所需的文字會傳送至 OpenRouter，再由其依我們的 preset 轉送至選定的下游 AI 模型供應商。各供應商會依其自身的隱私與資料保留政策處理並可能保留資料。

  • 除上述服務供應商外，我們不會向任何第三方揭露您的資料，但法律另有規定者除外。

7. 資料保留與刪除

  • 我們會保留您的資料，直至您自行刪除或刪除帳戶為止。
  • 您可隨時在「設定 → 刪除帳戶」永久刪除帳戶及所有相關資料（包括記帳、單據、語音等）。此操作無法復原。
