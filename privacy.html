狗狗記賬 (Doggo Ledger) — Privacy Policy / 私隱政策

Last updated / 最後更新: 24 June 2026

English

Doggo Ledger ("the App") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, how we use and protect it, and your rights.

1. Information we collect

  • Account: the email address you provide if you choose to sign in; otherwise an anonymous account identifier is created automatically.
  • Ledger data: the expense / income amounts, categories, titles, notes, dates and savings goals you enter.
  • Receipt images: photos you capture or select to upload.
  • Voice clips: audio you record to log expenses.
  • Preferences: e.g. daily reminders, haptics.

2. How we use it

  • To provide the core bookkeeping, spending-statistics and pet-raising features.
  • To automatically recognise receipt images and voice via a third-party AI service (Google Gemini), turning them into ledger entries.
  • To sync your data across devices once you sign in.

3. Sharing

  • Cloud storage & processing: the App uses Supabase for backend storage; receipt images and voice clips are sent to Google Gemini for recognition.
  • We do not sell your personal data and do not use it for cross-app advertising tracking.
  • We do not disclose your data to any other third party except the service providers above, or where required by law.

4. Storage & security

  • Your data is stored in Supabase and protected by Row Level Security — each user can access only their own data.
  • All network traffic uses HTTPS / TLS encryption.

5. Retention & deletion

  • You can permanently delete your account and all related data (ledger, receipts, voice, etc.) at any time via Settings → Delete Account. This is irreversible.
  • You can also delete individual records.

6. Children

  • The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

7. Your rights

  • You may access, correct or delete your personal data. Contact us at the email below.

8. Updates

  • We may update this policy from time to time; material changes will be posted in the App or on this page.

9. Contact

  • Privacy questions: aspirineqq8@gmail.com

繁體中文

「狗狗記賬」（下稱「本應用程式」）重視你嘅私隱。本政策說明我哋會收集咩資料、點樣使用同保護，以及你有咩權利。

1. 我哋收集嘅資料

  • 帳戶資料： 當你選擇登入時提供嘅電郵地址；未登入時，系統會自動建立一個匿名帳戶識別碼。
  • 記賬資料： 你輸入嘅開支／收入金額、分類、標題、備註、時間，以及存錢目標。
  • 單據相片： 你影低或選取上載嘅單據圖片。
  • 語音片段： 你錄製用嚟記賬嘅語音。
  • 偏好設定： 例如每日提醒、震動回饋等。

2. 我哋點樣使用呢啲資料

  • 提供記賬、開支統計、養成狗狗等核心功能。
  • 透過第三方人工智能服務（Google Gemini）自動辨識單據相片同語音內容，轉換成記賬資料。
  • 喺你登入之後，跨裝置同步你嘅資料。

3. 資料分享

  • 雲端儲存與處理： 本應用程式使用 Supabase 作後端儲存；單據圖片同語音會傳送至 Google Gemini 作辨識用途。
  • 我哋唔會出售你嘅個人資料，亦唔會用作跨應用程式嘅廣告追蹤。
  • 除上述服務供應商外，我哋唔會向任何第三方披露你嘅資料，除非法律另有要求。

4. 資料儲存與保安

  • 你嘅資料儲存喺 Supabase 雲端，並受「列級安全（Row Level Security）」保護——每位用戶只可存取屬於自己嘅資料。
  • 所有網絡傳輸均使用 HTTPS／TLS 加密。

5. 資料保留與刪除

  • 你可以隨時喺「設定 → 刪除帳戶」永久刪除你嘅帳戶及所有相關資料（包括記賬、單據、語音等）。此操作無法復原。
  • 你亦可以逐筆刪除個別記錄。

6. 兒童私隱

  • 本應用程式並非針對 13 歲以下兒童設計，亦唔會故意收集兒童嘅個人資料。

7. 你嘅權利

  • 你有權查閱、更正或刪除你嘅個人資料。如需協助，請透過下方電郵聯絡我哋。

8. 政策更新

  • 我哋可能會不時更新本政策；如有重大變更，會喺本應用程式內或本頁面公佈。

9. 聯絡我哋

  • 如對私隱有任何疑問，請電郵：aspirineqq8@gmail.com
